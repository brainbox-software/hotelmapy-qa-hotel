Willkommen im Hotel Seeblick

A made-up hotel on a third-party origin, for the Hotelmapy test campaigns (#638). Each page in the menu embeds the map one way, the way a hotelier or their agency would paste it.

QA settings

Pages

  • One-tag snippet — the admin's snippet mid-page, plus a second one far below the fold (lazy loading, two frames on one page, fullscreen).
  • Container div — the tag in <head>, the empty data-hotelmapy-embed div in the body.
  • Behind Klaro — the two-part snippet, held back by Klaro until the visitor agrees.
  • Behind Cookiebot — the same snippet the guide gives for Cookiebot, held back until the visitor allows marketing cookies.
  • Click to load — the one-tag snippet for a map set to "Click to load"; the remembered click across pages and reloads.
  • Raw iframe — the public map URL in a hand-written iframe.
  • Legacy snippet — the Atlantik markup as it sits on the hotel's site today: one loader, four divs with legacy type strings.
